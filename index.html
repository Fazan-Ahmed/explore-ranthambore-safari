Planning your Ranthambore visit

A Ranthambore safari is a guided wildlife drive inside the national park in an open vehicle. Routes, zones and timings are managed by the park authorities and can change by season, so please confirm the latest information before booking.

Morning and afternoon shifts are usually offered. Mornings can be cold in winter and afternoons very hot in summer, so dress in layers and carry water, sunscreen and a hat.

Choose muted colours such as green, brown or khaki, carry valid photo ID for every guest, and follow your guide's instructions. Visitors are responsible for providing accurate guest details. No wildlife sighting can be guaranteed.

Getting to Ranthambore

Ranthambore National Park is near Sawai Madhopur in Rajasthan. Travel times and distances can vary, so please check the latest information before you travel.

Train

Sawai Madhopur Junction is the nearest major railway station and is well connected by rail.

Road

Sawai Madhopur is reachable by road from nearby Rajasthan cities. Check road and weather conditions before leaving.

Air

The nearest major airport is in Jaipur, from where you can continue by road or rail.

Frequently asked questions

How can I enquire about a Ranthambore safari?

Fill in the enquiry form on the Safari Booking page, message us on WhatsApp, or call us. Share your preferred date, number of guests, safari type and shift.

What is the difference between Gypsy and Canter safari?

A Gypsy is a smaller open jeep that suits families and small groups. A Canter is a larger open vehicle that seats more people and suits bigger groups. Please ask us for current seating and availability.

Can I request a particular safari date?

Yes. Tell us your preferred date and shift. We will check the available options, but we cannot promise any date or shift.

How early should I enquire?

As early as you can. Safari availability depends on the booking process and can change, so earlier enquiries usually give you more options.

What information is required for a safari enquiry?

Your name, mobile number, safari date, Nationality, number of guests, safari type (Gypsy or Canter) and preferred shift (morning or afternoon). Further details may be requested for booking.

What does your price include?

The price shown is the total per seat. It includes the Forest Department ticket charge, our booking assistance charge and applicable taxes. We confirm the total with you before you pay, and no other charge is added after that.

Can I contact you on WhatsApp?

Yes. Use the WhatsApp button on this website.

Is this the official Forest Department website?

No. Explore Ranthambore Safari is an independent private safari booking assistance service and is not the official website of the Rajasthan Forest Department or the Government of Rajasthan.

Is tiger sighting guaranteed?

No. No wildlife sighting can be guaranteed. Tigers and other animals are wild and move freely.

Can safari availability or pricing change?

Yes. Availability, prices, timings and rules can change. Please confirm the latest information before booking.

What happens after I submit the enquiry?

We review your details, check the available options and contact you by phone or WhatsApp. Submitting an enquiry is not a booking confirmation.

What is the cancellation/refund process?

Refund depends on how early you cancel: 75% of the eligible amount if 30 days or more before the safari date, 50% if between 72 hours and 30 days, and no refund within 72 hours. Some charges may be non-refundable. If we cannot confirm a booking after you have paid, the full amount is refunded. Please read our Refund Policy before paying.
