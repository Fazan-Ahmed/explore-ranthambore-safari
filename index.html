Privacy Policy

Information about how Explore Ranthambore Safari handles information provided by website visitors.

Last updated: October 2026

INTRODUCTION

Privacy Policy

Explore Ranthambore Safari is an independent private safari booking assistance service for visitors planning a trip to Ranthambore and Sawai Madhopur, Rajasthan, India.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use this website, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

We aim to collect only the information reasonably necessary to respond to safari enquiries and provide the requested assistance.

Explore Ranthambore Safari is not affiliated with the Forest Department or Government of Rajasthan.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Information We Collect

Depending on how you contact us, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide for a safari enquiry.

Information You May Provide

  • Your name.
  • Your mobile or telephone number.
  • Preferred safari date.
  • Number of guests.
  • Preferred safari type, such as Gypsy or Canter.
  • Preferred safari shift, where applicable.

If you contact us directly through phone or WhatsApp, the information you provide during that communication may also be available to us.

Website Technical Information

Like many websites, basic technical information may be processed by the hosting provider or other technical services used to operate the website. This may include information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages and basic access times.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information through the website.

USE OF INFORMATION

How We Use Information

Information provided through a safari enquiry may be used for purposes such as:

  • Responding to your safari enquiry.
  • Understanding your requested safari date and requirements.
  • Communicating available safari options.
  • Contacting you regarding your enquiry.
  • Providing information related to your requested service.
  • Maintaining and improving the website and its functionality.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

EXTERNAL SERVICES

WhatsApp and Third-Party Services

This website provides a WhatsApp contact option for visitor enquiries. When you choose to contact us through WhatsApp, your communication is handled through WhatsApp and is subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy practices.

The website may also use third-party technical services in the future, such as form-processing, analytics, hosting, security or advertising services.

If additional third-party services that process personal information are added, this Privacy Policy should be updated to accurately describe their use.

DATA SECURITY

Data Security

Reasonable steps are taken to protect information received through the website and related communication channels from unauthorized access, misuse or disclosure.

The website is intended to be served over HTTPS when deployed on the production domain.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Visitors should avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information through website forms, WhatsApp or other communication channels.

DATA RETENTION

How Long We Keep Information

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to an enquiry, manage a booking-related communication, maintain business records, resolve disputes or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Information that is no longer reasonably required may be deleted or otherwise disposed of when appropriate.

YOUR RIGHTS

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning the personal information you have provided to us.

  • Request information about personal data we may hold about you.
  • Ask for inaccurate information to be corrected.
  • Request deletion of information where applicable.
  • Ask questions about how your information is being used.
  • Choose not to provide information that is not necessary for your enquiry.

Some requests may be subject to legal, operational or record-keeping requirements.

THIRD-PARTY LINKS

Third-Party Links

This website may contain links to third-party websites or services, including external communication or information platforms.

These third-party websites operate independently and may have their own privacy policies, terms and data practices.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing the relevant privacy policy before providing personal information to an external service.

COOKIES & ANALYTICS

Cookies and Analytics

The website may use essential browser technologies or cookies required for basic functionality.

If analytics, Google Ads conversion tracking, Google Analytics or another tracking service is added to the website, the Privacy Policy and relevant website disclosures should be updated to accurately describe that technology and its purpose.

No analytics or advertising tracking should be described here as active unless it has actually been installed on the website.

POLICY UPDATES

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time when website functionality, services, communication methods or applicable requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page together with an updated date.
