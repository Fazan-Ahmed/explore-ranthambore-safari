How to Book a Ranthambore Safari: Step by Step

By Explore Ranthambore Safari team · Published: 15 November 2026 · Last updated: 16 November 2026

Ranthambore National Park, near Sawai Madhopur in Rajasthan, is one of India's best-known places to see Bengal tigers in the wild. Safari tickets are issued by the Rajasthan Forest Department through its booking system, and availability can change quickly in the busy season. Advance booking opens about 3 months before your safari date. This guide explains the whole booking process in simple steps.

Step 1: Decide your date, vehicle and shift

Choose the date you plan to visit, the vehicle type (Gypsy or Canter) and the shift (morning or afternoon). If you are not sure which vehicle suits you, read our guide on Gypsy vs Canter safari.

Step 2: Know when booking opens

Advance booking opens about 3 months before the safari date. For a busy-season date, it is best to book as soon as your date becomes available, because the most popular dates and shifts fill up first. Booking rules can change, so confirm the current schedule with the Forest Department booking system or with us.

Step 3: Keep your details ready

  • Safari date and preferred shift
  • Number of guests
  • Nationality of the guests (Indian or foreign national)
  • Safari type: Gypsy or Canter
  • A valid government photo ID for every guest

Step 4: Book yourself or take assistance

You can book directly through the Forest Department's official booking system. If you prefer help, an independent agency such as ours can check availability and handle the booking on your behalf. We are a private agency and not the Forest Department.

Step 5: Check availability and the total price

Availability depends on the date, shift and vehicle. If you book through us, we confirm the available option and the total price in writing (phone or WhatsApp) before you pay. The total includes the Forest Department ticket charge, our booking assistance charge and applicable taxes.

Step 6: Pay only after you understand the terms

Read the cancellation and refund terms before paying. A booking is confirmed only after payment is received and confirmation is communicated to you. An enquiry on its own is not a confirmed booking.

Step 7: Carry the same ID on the day of the safari

The same valid government photo ID used for the booking must be carried on the day of the safari by every guest. Safari timings change with the season, so check your ticket for the exact timing.

Understand the booking quotas

Depending on how close your safari date is, tickets are offered under different quotas: advance booking, current (last-minute) booking, and tatkal booking for a full Gypsy. Availability and rules are set by the Forest Department. You can see how each option works on our Safari Packages page.

After you book: what to expect

  • Keep your booking confirmation safe on your phone.
  • Reach the designated boarding point at the reporting time mentioned on your confirmed safari permit.
  • Carry the same government photo ID that was used for the booking.
  • Hotel pick-up and drop is not included in a safari ticket, so plan your transfer in advance.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • ID mismatch: carrying a different ID from the one used for the booking.
  • Expecting a fixed zone: zones are allotted by the Forest Department, so a particular zone cannot be guaranteed.
  • Paying before the price is clear: ask for the total price in writing before you pay.
  • Waiting too long in the busy season: popular dates fill up quickly once booking opens.
  • Using a site that does not say who it is: check that the website clearly states whether it is the official portal or an independent agency.

Quick booking checklist

  • Date and shift decided
  • Gypsy or Canter chosen
  • Number of guests and nationality noted
  • Valid photo ID ready for every guest
  • Total price and cancellation terms checked
  • Confirmation received after payment

Things to keep in mind

  • Zones, vehicles and routes are allotted by the Forest Department, so a particular zone or date cannot be guaranteed.
  • Wildlife sightings cannot be guaranteed because the animals are wild and move freely.
  • Hotel pick-up and drop is not included in a safari ticket.

You can also read the season-wise safari timings and the safari zones guide on our home page.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many months in advance can I book a Ranthambore safari?

Advance booking opens about 3 months before the safari date. Rules can change, so confirm the current schedule with the Forest Department booking system or with us before you plan.

How early should I book a Ranthambore safari?

Availability changes quickly in the busy season, so it is best to book as soon as advance booking opens for your date. Exact dates and availability are decided by the Forest Department booking system.

Can I book a Ranthambore safari on the same day?

Current (last-minute) booking may be possible depending on availability, but options are limited. Booking in advance is safer, especially in the busy season.

Is an enquiry the same as a confirmed booking?

No. An enquiry only lets us check availability and contact you. A booking is confirmed only after payment is received and confirmation is communicated to you.

Which ID do I need for the safari?

Every guest needs a valid government photo ID, and the same ID used for the booking must be carried on the day of the safari.

Can I choose my safari zone?

Zones are allotted through the Forest Department's booking system, so a particular zone, vehicle or date cannot be guaranteed.
