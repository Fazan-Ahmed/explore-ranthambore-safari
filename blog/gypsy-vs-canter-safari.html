Gypsy vs Canter Safari in Ranthambore: Which One to Choose?

By Explore Ranthambore Safari team · Published: 15 November 2026 · Last updated: 15 November 2026

Ranthambore offers safaris in two types of open vehicles: the Gypsy (jeep) and the Canter. Both enter the park with a guide in a morning or afternoon shift. The right choice mostly depends on the size of your group.

Gypsy (jeep) safari

  • Open 6-seater vehicle.
  • Best for families and small groups.
  • Maximum 6 persons and 2 children (1 to 5 years).
  • A smaller group means a more relaxed and flexible experience inside the vehicle.

Canter safari

  • Open 20-seater vehicle.
  • A group option that suits larger families and parties.
  • Seats are shared by guests in the group, so it works well when many people travel together.

How to choose

  • Up to 6 people: a Gypsy is usually the simpler choice.
  • A big group travelling together: a Canter keeps everyone in one vehicle.
  • Children: check the child rule for the Gypsy (maximum 2 children aged 1 to 5 years) before booking.

Important notes

Availability, zones and vehicles are decided by the Forest Department, so a particular vehicle, zone or date cannot be guaranteed. Valid government photo ID is required for every guest. No wildlife sighting can be guaranteed in any vehicle or season.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many people can sit in a Gypsy?

A Gypsy is an open 6-seater jeep. Maximum 6 persons and 2 children (1 to 5 years).

How many people can sit in a Canter?

A Canter is an open 20-seater vehicle suited to larger groups.

Can I choose a particular zone with a Gypsy or Canter?

Zones are allotted through the Forest Department's booking system, so we cannot guarantee a particular zone, vehicle or date.

Is tiger sighting guaranteed in a Gypsy or Canter?

No. Wildlife sightings cannot be guaranteed because the animals are wild and move freely.
