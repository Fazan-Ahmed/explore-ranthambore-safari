Privacy Policy

How Explore Ranthambore Safari collects, uses and protects your information when you enquire about or book a Ranthambore safari.

Independent Safari Booking Assistance — We are a private service and are not affiliated with the Forest Department or Government of Rajasthan.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Explore Ranthambore Safari (“we”, “us”, “our”) is an independent, privately operated safari booking assistance service based in Sawai Madhopur, Rajasthan. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website, send a safari enquiry, contact us or proceed with a booking, and how we use, share and protect it. By using the website and our services you agree to this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to respond to your enquiry and to assist with your booking.

A. Personal Data

When you submit the enquiry form, request a quote, call us or message us on WhatsApp, we may ask for information that can be used to contact or identify you, including:

  • Full name (for you and your tour members)
  • Mobile number (including WhatsApp number)
  • Email address, where provided
  • Preferred safari date, shift and number of guests
  • Preferred safari type (Gypsy or Canter)
  • Identification details required by the booking process, for an actual booking

B. Enquiry, Booking and Payment Data

Details about the safari you enquire about or book (such as Gypsy or Canter, shift and zone preference where applicable) and, where applicable, payment confirmation details such as a transaction reference. We do not collect or store card details through this website.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiry: to review your request and contact you by phone or WhatsApp.
  • For communication: to send updates, confirmations, safari timings and answers to your questions.
  • To assist with booking: where you choose to proceed, to coordinate the booking, including passing the required booking information to the applicable authorities or service providers.
  • Records and compliance: to keep booking and payment records, resolve disputes and comply with applicable legal and booking-process requirements.

By submitting an enquiry you agree that we may contact you by phone or WhatsApp about it.

4. How the Enquiry Form Works

The enquiry form sends your details to us by email through EmailJS, a third-party email service. EmailJS processes the form data only to deliver it to us. WhatsApp messages are handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy policy.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share necessary information only as follows:

  • Authorities and booking systems (including the Forest Department): where a safari booking requires it, the relevant booking or identification information may be shared with the applicable authorities or authorised booking systems in line with their requirements.
  • Service providers: safari operators, drivers, guides, payment providers or other providers involved in fulfilling your booking request.
  • Legal requirements: where required by law, to protect our rights, or to respond to lawful requests from government agencies.

6. Cookies and Tracking

We do not currently use analytics or advertising cookies or tracking tags on this website. If we add tools such as Google Analytics or Google Ads conversion tracking, we will update this policy first. Your browser may store basic technical data needed for the website to work, and you can manage cookies in your browser settings.

7. Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable security measures, including HTTPS, to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. We keep your information only as long as needed for your enquiry, booking records, support and legal requirements.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of incorrect data.
  • Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal or booking retention requirements).

9. Children

This website is meant for adults planning a visit. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Names of child guests are collected only from a parent or guardian for a booking.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services, such as WhatsApp, that we do not own or control. We are not responsible for their privacy practices or content, and we encourage you to read their privacy policies.

11. Independent Service Disclosure

Explore Ranthambore Safari is an independent private safari booking assistance service. We are not the Rajasthan Forest Department, the Government of Rajasthan or an official government website, and we do not claim any government ownership, endorsement or affiliation.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is published on this page with the “Last updated” date. See also our Terms & Conditions and Refund Policy.
