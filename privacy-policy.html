1. Introduction

Explore Ranthambore Safari (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, submit a lead or booking form, or use our travel planning services in Ranthambore.

By accessing our website or submitting your information to us, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

  • Personal Details: Name, email address, phone number, city, and country of residence.
  • Travel Preferences: Dates of travel, group size, safari preference (Jeep or Canter), hotel choices, and special travel requests.
  • Government Identity Proofs (Only when required for bookings): Full names, ages, genders, and government-issued ID details (e.g., Passport, Aadhaar, Voter ID, or Driver’s License) strictly required for official government safari permit registrations and hotel check-ins.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect non-personal browser details through standard web analytics tools:

We use the collected information for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Responding to your inquiries and delivering customized tour quotes for safaris, fort visits and museum tours.
  • Processing and coordinating safari permit registrations, hotel bookings, and transportation arrangements.
  • Communicating important trip updates, itinerary details, or changes in travel schedules via phone, email, or messaging services.
  • Improving website functionality, user experience, and overall customer service.
  • Complying with legal requirements and local regulations.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or commercial purposes.
