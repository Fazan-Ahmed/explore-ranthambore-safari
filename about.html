About Explore Ranthambore Safari

Explore Ranthambore Safari is an independently operated private safari booking assistance service based in Sawai Madhopur, Rajasthan, India.

We help travellers plan their visit to Ranthambore by giving information and assistance with safari enquiries and booking requests.

Important: Explore Ranthambore Safari is not a government organisation, is not the Rajasthan Forest Department, and is not the official website or authority of Ranthambore National Park. We are not affiliated with or representatives of any government authority.

What We Do

We assist travellers with their Ranthambore safari requirements, including:

  • Safari booking assistance
  • Safari permit and booking-related assistance
  • Information about Gypsy and Canter safari options and booking procedures
  • Phone and WhatsApp support for your enquiry

Our role is to assist you with your booking. Safari permits, vehicle availability, safari zones, entry and other park-related services remain subject to the applicable booking system, availability and rules of the relevant authorities.

How We Assist With Your Safari Enquiry

  1. Share your requirements. Tell us your preferred safari date, shift, number of guests and safari type.
  2. Enquiry and availability. We check the relevant available options and give you the applicable information.
  3. Confirmation details. We tell you the available option, the total price and the applicable terms before you pay.
  4. Booking and visit. Once the applicable booking process is completed and confirmed, you can continue planning your Ranthambore visit.

Our Approach

We aim to give customers:

  • Clear information about our service
  • Transparent pricing and applicable charges
  • Accurate booking conditions
  • Clear information about what is and is not included in a booking
  • Responsive customer support

Submitting an enquiry or booking request does not guarantee confirmation. A safari booking is confirmed only after the applicable booking and payment requirements are completed and confirmation has been communicated to you.

Safari Booking Information

Safari availability depends on the selected date, safari type, vehicle and zone availability and the applicable booking conditions.

Requesting a particular safari zone, vehicle or timing does not guarantee it. Availability remains subject to the applicable booking system and the relevant authorities.

Where a booking needs information, permits or services from an authority or third-party provider, we assist with the applicable booking process.

Wildlife sightings are naturally unpredictable. We do not guarantee tiger sightings or sightings of any particular animal during a safari.

Pricing, Payment and Refunds

  • Starting prices are Gypsy from ₹2,250 per person and Canter from ₹1,290 per person, including permit and all applicable charges. The total amount payable is told to you before you pay, and we do not charge a separate service fee.
  • Payment is requested only after the availability, total price and booking conditions have been communicated to you, and is accepted through UPI or another method communicated before payment.
  • If we cannot confirm your booking after receiving payment, the full amount received is refunded.
  • For a confirmed booking you cancel: 75% refund 30 days or more before the safari date, 50% between 72 hours and 30 days, and no refund within 72 hours.

Please review the Refund Policy and Terms & Conditions before making any payment.
