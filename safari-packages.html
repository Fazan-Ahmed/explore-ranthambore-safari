Ranthambore Safari Packages & Booking Quotas

Choose your vehicle and your booking quota for the 2026-27 season. We are an independent private agency and help you request tickets through the Rajasthan Forest Department's booking system.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Choose Your Safari Vehicle

Gypsy Safari

Open 6-seater jeep

  • Suits families and small groups.
  • Maximum 6 persons and 2 children (1 to 5 years).
  • A smaller group makes the drive easier to enjoy and to photograph from.

Canter Safari

Open 20-seater vehicle

  • Suits larger families, school groups and parties.
  • A practical option when many people travel together.
  • Higher seating gives a wider view of the surroundings.

Before you choose

  • Valid government photo ID is required for every guest.
  • Zones, vehicles and routes are allotted by the Forest Department.
  • Hotel pick-up and drop is not included in a safari ticket.
  • No wildlife sighting can be guaranteed.

Advance Booking Quota

Recommended for planned trips. Book ahead for your chosen date, shift and vehicle.

  • Gypsy (Indian guests): ₹2200 per seat
  • Canter (Indian guests): ₹1400 per seat
  • Gypsy (foreign guests): ₹4100 per seat
  • Canter (foreign guests): ₹3300 per seat
  • Booking opens in advance, so you get more date and shift options.
  • ​Standard 55% advance payment model.45% after ticket confirmation
  • Zone and vehicle are allotted by the Forest Department. Highest chances of preferred zone.
  • Book Up to 365 Days in Advance

Current Booking Quota (Last Minute)

For travellers who decide close to the safari date. Availability is checked in real time.

  • Gypsy (Indian guests): ₹2600 per seat
  • Canter (Indian guests): ₹1900 per seat
  • Gypsy (foreign guests): ₹5050 per seat
  • Canter (foreign guests): ₹4600 per seat
  • Subject to availability on the day.
  • Zone and vehicle cannot be chosen or guaranteed.

Tatkal Booking Quota (Full Gypsy)

A full Gypsy for up to 6 persons, for guests who want to book a whole vehicle at short notice.

  • Full Gypsy (Indian guests): ₹29,000 per Gypsy
  • Full Gypsy (foreign guests): ₹44,500 per Gypsy
  • Tatkal Booking Opens 7 Days Before Safari
  • No Refunds or Transfers Allowed.
  • Government Premium Fees Included.
  • Please read the cancellation and refund terms for this quota in our Terms & Conditions before paying.

What the Price Includes

  • The total includes the Forest Department ticket charge, our booking assistance charge and applicable taxes.
  • A booking is confirmed only after payment is received and we confirm to you. An enquiry is not a confirmed booking.
  • Official charges can change. The price we confirm in writing is the one that applies.
