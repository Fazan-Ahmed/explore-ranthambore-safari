How Ranthambore Safari Booking Works

  1. Share your travel details. Tell us your preferred safari date, number of guests, safari type and preferred shift.
  2. We check the available options. We review the information applicable to your enquiry.
  3. We contact you. We explain the available option and the total price by phone or WhatsApp.
  4. Confirm before payment. Any booking and payment is handled according to the applicable booking terms. Please review the details before making payment.

Pricing, Payment, Cancellation and Refund

  • Starting prices: Gypsy from ₹2,250 per person and Canter from ₹1,290 per person, including permit and all applicable charges. The final price depends on the date, zone and availability.
  • The total amount payable is told to you before you pay. We do not charge a separate service fee.
  • 30 days or more before the safari date: 75% refund of the eligible amount.
  • Between 72 hours and 30 days before: 50% refund.
  • Within 72 hours: no refund.
  • If we cannot confirm your booking after receiving payment, you get a full refund of the amount paid.

Some government or payment charges may be non-refundable. See the full Refund Policy and Terms & Conditions.

How Payment Works

  • Payment is requested only after the availability, total price and booking conditions have been communicated to you.
  • Payments are accepted through UPI or another payment method communicated to you before payment. Please check the payee name and amount before you pay.
  • A booking request is not processed until payment has been received, and a booking is confirmed only after confirmation is communicated to you.

Planning Your Ranthambore Safari

A Ranthambore safari is a guided wildlife experience inside the national park. Safari routes, zones, timings and other operational matters are subject to the applicable park authorities and booking system.

Visitors should carry the identification and other documents required for their booking and follow the instructions of the safari staff and guide.

Wildlife sightings are naturally unpredictable. No tiger, leopard or other wildlife sighting can be guaranteed.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I enquire about a Ranthambore safari?

You can use the enquiry form on this page, contact us by WhatsApp or call us. Share your preferred date, number of guests and safari type.

What is the difference between Gypsy and Canter safari?

A Gypsy is a smaller open safari vehicle generally used for smaller groups. A Canter is a larger open vehicle generally suitable for larger groups. Current availability should be confirmed before booking.

How early should I enquire?

You can enquire as early as possible because availability depends on the applicable booking system and can change.

What information is needed for a safari enquiry?

We generally need your preferred date, number of guests, safari type and preferred shift. Additional information may be required under the applicable booking process.

Do you charge a service fee?

No. We do not charge a separate service fee. The total amount payable is told to you before you pay.

Can I contact you on WhatsApp?

Yes. You can use the WhatsApp button on this website to contact us regarding your safari enquiry.

Is this the official Rajasthan Forest Department website?

No. Explore Ranthambore Safari is an independent private safari booking assistance service and is not the official website of the Rajasthan Forest Department or the Government of Rajasthan.

Is tiger sighting guaranteed?

No. Wildlife sightings cannot be guaranteed because animals move freely in their natural environment.

Can safari availability and pricing change?

Yes. Availability, pricing, timings, zones and applicable requirements may change. Please confirm the latest applicable information before booking.

Is submitting the enquiry a confirmed booking?

No. Submitting an enquiry only allows us to review your request and contact you about available options. A booking is confirmed only according to the applicable booking process.

How does cancellation and refund work?

30 days or more before the safari date: 75% refund of the eligible amount. Between 72 hours and 30 days: 50% refund. Within 72 hours: no refund. Some charges may be non-refundable. If we cannot confirm a booking after you have paid, the full amount is refunded. Please read our Refund Policy before making payment.
