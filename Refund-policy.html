Cancellation & Refund Information

Refund Policy

Please review the cancellation and refund conditions before proceeding with a safari booking.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Safari Cancellation

Cancellation of a confirmed safari booking is subject to the cancellation conditions applicable to that booking.

Safari bookings may be subject to specific cancellation rules, deadlines and conditions determined by the applicable booking authority, operator or service provider.

Because cancellation conditions can vary depending on the booking, customers should confirm the applicable cancellation terms before making payment.

2. Booking Information

An enquiry submitted through the website, phone or WhatsApp does not by itself constitute a confirmed safari booking.

A booking is considered confirmed only after the applicable booking process has been completed and confirmation has been communicated.

Customers should carefully review the safari date, number of guests, safari type and other booking details before confirming the booking.

3. Cancellation Request

If a customer wishes to cancel a confirmed safari booking, the cancellation request should be made as soon as possible through the available contact method.

A cancellation request is not automatically treated as a completed cancellation until it has been acknowledged or processed according to the applicable booking conditions.

Customers should provide the relevant booking details when requesting cancellation so that the booking can be identified correctly.

4. Weather and Natural Conditions

Safari operations may be affected by weather conditions, park operating conditions, safety considerations or other natural circumstances.

Weather conditions do not automatically create a refund entitlement unless the applicable booking terms provide for a refund or alternative arrangement.

Where a safari is cancelled or affected by the applicable authority or operator, the outcome will depend on the rules and conditions applicable to that booking.

5. Wildlife Sightings

Wildlife sightings are naturally unpredictable.

No tiger, leopard, bear or other wildlife sighting is guaranteed.

Failure to see a particular animal or wildlife species during a safari does not by itself qualify as a reason for a refund.

6. Refund Policy

Refund eligibility depends on the applicable booking terms, cancellation conditions and status of the relevant safari booking.

There is no universal refund amount applicable to every safari booking.

Where a refund is applicable, the amount and method of refund will depend on the conditions applicable to the relevant booking.

Any applicable non-refundable charges, service charges or other amounts specified before booking may be deducted where permitted by the applicable terms.

7. Safari Cancellation & Refund

Safari permit cancellation and refund will be subject to the applicable rules of the Rajasthan Forest Department and the relevant safari booking system.

The currently applicable cancellation schedule is:

  • Cancellation 30 days or more before the visit date: 75% refund of the eligible amount.
  • Cancellation between 72 hours and 30 days before the visit date: 50% refund of the eligible amount.
  • Cancellation within 72 hours of the visit date: No refund.

The refundable amount is subject to the applicable booking conditions. Certain government, permit, vehicle, guide, processing, service or other applicable charges may be non-refundable, where applicable.

Important: The refund applicable to a booking will be determined according to the rules and conditions applicable to that booking at the time of cancellation.

Ranthambore Ticket Booking is an independent safari booking assistance service and is not the official website of the Rajasthan Forest Department.

8. Customer Information

Customers are responsible for providing accurate information during the enquiry and booking process.

  • Correct guest name and details.
  • Correct safari date.
  • Correct number of guests.
  • Correct contact information.

Refund or cancellation requests caused by incorrect information supplied by the customer may be subject to the applicable booking conditions.

9. Availability and Permit Allocation

Safari availability, vehicle allocation, zones, permits and related arrangements may depend on the applicable booking system and authority.

We cannot guarantee a particular zone, vehicle, permit allocation or wildlife sighting unless it has been specifically confirmed as part of the applicable booking.

If availability or allocation changes due to the applicable authority or operator, the applicable booking rules will determine whether a refund, alternative arrangement or other resolution is available.

10. How to Request a Refund or Cancellation

Customers who need to request a cancellation or refund should contact us using the available phone or WhatsApp contact details.

Please provide the relevant booking information so that the request can be identified and reviewed.

Refund requests will be reviewed according to the applicable booking conditions.

Call 8619152322 WhatsApp Us

11. Refund Processing

Where a refund is approved, the processing method and timeframe may depend on the payment method, applicable booking provider and processing requirements.

A refund may not be immediate after a cancellation request is submitted.

Customers will be informed of the applicable refund process where a refund is available.

12. Situations Where a Refund May Not Be Available

Depending on the applicable booking conditions, a refund may not be available in circumstances such as:

  • Cancellation outside an applicable cancellation period.
  • Failure to attend a confirmed safari without an eligible cancellation.
  • Incorrect customer information that prevents the booking from being used.
  • Failure to meet applicable identification or entry requirements.
  • Absence of a desired wildlife sighting.

The actual outcome will depend on the specific terms applicable to the confirmed booking.

13. Changes or Cancellation by Authorities or Operators

Safari operations may be changed, restricted or cancelled by the applicable authorities or operators because of operational, administrative, weather, safety or other conditions.

If such a situation occurs, any refund or alternative arrangement will be handled according to the applicable booking conditions.

14. Policy Updates

This Refund Policy may be updated from time to time when the website, booking process, services or applicable requirements change.

The latest version published on this page will apply to future bookings and enquiries, subject to the terms applicable to an already confirmed booking.

The "Last updated" date shown at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
