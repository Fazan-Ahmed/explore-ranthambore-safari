3. Booking Process

  1. Enquiry: you share your preferred date, shift, safari type and number of guests.
  2. Availability: we check and tell you the available options. Availability is not guaranteed.
  3. Price: the total price and booking conditions are communicated before you pay.
  4. Confirmation: an enquiry, booking request or chat is not a confirmed booking. A booking is confirmed only after the booking process is completed, any applicable payment is received and confirmation is communicated to you.

4. Charges and Payment

Prices depend on safari type, date, availability and the rules in effect at the time, and are not guaranteed unless confirmed for your booking. Starting prices shown on the website (Gypsy from ₹2,250 per person, Canter from ₹1,290 per person, last checked October 2026) include the permit and applicable charges, and the final price depends on the date, zone and availability. The total amount payable may include the safari permit or applicable government charges, and vehicle and guide charges. It is communicated clearly before payment. We do not charge a separate service fee.

Payment is requested only after the availability, total price and booking conditions have been communicated to you. Payments are accepted through UPI or another payment method communicated to you before payment. A booking request is not processed until payment has been received.

Please verify the amount, payee details and booking details before you pay. Payment records may be kept for booking records, support, dispute resolution and legal requirements.

5. Customer Responsibility

You must provide complete and accurate information for the booking, which may include:

  • Full name of each guest
  • Age, gender and nationality
  • Valid government ID details
  • Mobile number
  • Email address, where provided

Incorrect or incomplete information may lead to booking rejection, cancellation or denial of entry under the applicable booking or park rules.

6. Identity Verification

Visitors must carry the same valid government-issued ID that was used for the booking when entering Ranthambore National Park. Entry is subject to the applicable identification and park entry requirements, and we cannot guarantee entry if identification or information does not meet them.

7. Safari Confirmation and Availability

Confirmation depends on permit availability, booking-system availability and applicable park rules, and we cannot guarantee confirmation of every request. Availability can change without notice because of park instructions, weather, administrative decisions or other circumstances. A safari option is not held for you until payment and confirmation are completed.

Timings, zones and vehicle allocation are set by the authorities and may change. We do not guarantee a particular zone, vehicle, timing or date unless specifically confirmed for your booking.

8. Changes Requested by Customers

After a booking request is submitted, changes may be limited:

  • Date changes may not be possible.
  • Zone changes depend entirely on availability.
  • Name corrections may be restricted by Forest Department policies.

9. Cancellation and Refunds

Cancellations and refunds are governed by our Refund Policy:

  • 30 days or more before the safari date: 75% refund of the eligible amount.
  • Between 72 hours and 30 days before: 50% refund.
  • Within 72 hours or no-show: no refund.
  • If we cannot confirm a booking after receiving your payment, the full amount received is refunded.

Some government, permit, vehicle, guide or payment charges may be non-refundable when you cancel a confirmed booking. Please read the Refund Policy before you pay.

10. Customer Errors

No refund is provided if a booking fails because of customer mistakes, including:

  • Incorrect personal details.
  • Incorrect government ID information.
  • Failure to report on time.
  • Failure to carry required documents.

11. Wildlife Sightings

Ranthambore National Park is a natural wildlife habitat, and wildlife movement is natural and beyond anyone's control. No tiger, leopard, bear or other wildlife sighting is guaranteed.

12. Park Rules and Visitor Conduct

Every visitor must follow the instructions and rules issued by the Rajasthan Forest Department, park authorities, safari guides and drivers, including carrying valid photo ID and your booking confirmation, not disturbing wildlife, and following the photography and vehicle rules.

Violation of park rules may result in denial of entry, cancellation of the safari or other action by the park authorities. You are responsible for following the rules in force on the date of your safari.
