2. Safari Bookings & Government Regulations

  1. Permit Allocation:All safari permits (Jeep and Canter) in Ranthambore National Park are issued directly by the Rajasthan Forest Department through its official online system. Safari zones, vehicles, and guide assignments are strictly allocated by forest authorities at their sole discretion.
  2. Booking Details: Guests must provide accurate personal details (full legal name, age, gender, and government-issued photo ID details matching physical IDs) at the time of booking. Any discrepancies may result in permit rejection by forest officials at the gate.
  3. Non-Transferable & Non-Refundable Permits: Once issued by the Forest Department, safari permits are non-transferable and subject to official forest rules regarding modifications and refunds.

3. Payment Terms & Price Transparency

  1. Quotations: Tour package quotes provided by Explore Ranthambore Safari include our travel service fees, itinerary planning, logistics coordination, and applicable taxes.
  2. Payment Deadlines: Advance payment is required to confirm itinerary bookings and secure third-party arrangements (such as hotel rooms and private vehicles). Full payment schedules will be outlined in your official booking itinerary.
  3. Price Inclusions: All inclusions, exclusions, and fee breakdowns will be explicitly stated in your written booking confirmation. There are no hidden service charges.

4. Cancellation and Refunds Policy

Cancellation policies vary based on the specific travel components included in your package:

  • Safari Permits: Governed strictly by the Rajasthan Forest Department rules and guidelines

  • Complete terms regarding modifications, cancellations, and refund processing timelines are detailed on our dedicated Cancellation & Refund Policy page.
