Cancellation & Refund Information

Refund Policy

Please read the cancellation and refund conditions before you proceed with a safari booking.

Independent Safari Booking Assistance — We are a private service and are not affiliated with the Forest Department or Government of Rajasthan.

Last updated: October 2026

Quick summary

  • An enquiry (form, call or WhatsApp) is not a confirmed booking.
  • 30 days or more before the safari: 75% refund. Between 72 hours and 30 days: 50% refund. Within 72 hours: no refund.
  • If we cannot confirm your booking after you have paid, you get a full refund of the amount paid.
  • We do not charge a separate service fee.
  • No tiger or other wildlife sighting is guaranteed, and a missed sighting is not a reason for refund.
  • We are an independent private service, not the Forest Department.

1. About This Policy

Explore Ranthambore Safari is an independent private safari booking assistance service for visitors to Ranthambore and Sawai Madhopur. We are not the official website of the Rajasthan Forest Department or the Government of Rajasthan.

Safari permits and bookings are subject to availability and to the rules of the Rajasthan Forest Department and the relevant booking system, as applicable at the time of cancellation. This policy explains the cancellation and refund process for safari bookings handled through our service.

2. When a Booking Is Confirmed

Submitting an enquiry through our website, WhatsApp, email or phone does not by itself create a confirmed safari booking.

A booking is confirmed only after availability and booking information are completed, any applicable payment is received, and confirmation is communicated to you. Availability is not guaranteed until then.

The total amount payable is communicated before you pay. We do not charge a separate service fee.

3. Cancellation and Refund Schedule

For a confirmed safari booking that you cancel, the refund is:

  • 30 days or more before the safari date: 75% refund of the eligible amount.
  • More than 72 hours but less than 30 days before the safari date: 50% refund of the eligible amount.
  • 72 hours or less before the safari date, or no-show: no refund.

The “eligible amount” is the amount paid for the safari booking, excluding charges that cannot be refunded (see Section 4). If the Forest Department or the booking system changes its cancellation rules, the rules applicable at the time of cancellation will apply.

4. Non-Refundable Charges

When you cancel a confirmed booking, the following may be non-refundable. Any such charge is communicated before payment:

  • Payment processing charges, if any.
  • Government, permit, vehicle or guide charges that the authority or operator does not refund.

5. If We Cannot Confirm Your Booking

If we have received payment from you but cannot confirm or complete the safari booking, because of permit unavailability, booking-system availability or any other reason on our side, the full amount received for that unconfirmed booking will be refunded, with no deduction.

The refund is sent through the original payment method. The time it takes to reach your account may vary depending on your payment method and bank.

6. How to Request a Cancellation or Refund

Contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email as early as possible and share:

  • Customer name
  • Booking or enquiry reference, if available
  • Safari date and shift
  • Contact number
  • Reason for cancellation
  • Payment or booking details, where required

A cancellation is treated as completed only after we acknowledge it. The refund percentage is based on the date and time we receive your request. We will review it and tell you the applicable refund process.

7. Refund Processing

Where a refund is applicable, it is sent to the original payment method. Refunds are typically completed within 7–10 working days after approval. The exact time can vary depending on your bank, payment provider and the booking authority.

8. Weather, Natural Conditions and Changes by Authorities

Safari operations may be affected by weather, forest conditions, safety restrictions, government directions or other circumstances beyond our control, and may be changed, restricted or cancelled by the authorities or operators.

If a safari is cancelled or affected for such reasons, any refund or alternative date will follow the rules of the relevant authority or booking system, and we will help you with the available options. Weather alone does not create a refund entitlement unless the applicable rules provide for one.

9. Wildlife Sightings

Wildlife movement is natural and unpredictable. We do not guarantee any tiger sighting or sighting of any particular animal. A safari booking provides access to the safari experience, subject to the applicable rules and availability, and not a particular sighting. Not seeing an animal is not a reason for refund.

10. Third-Party Services

Where a booking involves a safari operator, driver, guide or other third-party provider, the cancellation and refund conditions of that provider may also apply. We will communicate the relevant terms wherever applicable.

11. Customer Information

You are responsible for giving accurate information for your booking, including:

  • Full name and guest names
  • Contact number
  • Safari date and shift
  • Number of guests
  • Age, gender, nationality and government ID details required by the booking process

Incorrect or incomplete information may affect the booking or any cancellation or refund.

12. Availability and Permit Allocation

Safari availability, permit issuance, zone, vehicle and guide allocation and other operational matters depend on the applicable booking system and the relevant authorities. We cannot guarantee a particular zone, vehicle or permit unless it is specifically confirmed for your booking.

13. Situations Where a Refund Is Not Available

  • Cancellation of a confirmed booking within 72 hours of the safari date.
  • Not attending a confirmed safari (no-show).
  • Incorrect guest, date or contact details supplied by the customer.
  • Not carrying valid identification or not meeting entry requirements.
  • Not seeing a particular animal.

14. Policy Updates

We may update this policy when our services, booking procedures or applicable rules change. The version published here applies to new bookings. A confirmed booking is handled under the policy that applied when it was confirmed. The “Last updated” date shows the latest revision.
